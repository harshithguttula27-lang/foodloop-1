Why FoodLoop exists

We built the app we wished existed on a Tuesday night.

FoodLoop started as a simple question: why does finding dinner take longer than eating it? We combined restaurant discovery, real menu data, and order tracking into a single loop — so you spend less time deciding and more time eating.

We work directly with local kitchens, not just national chains, so the prices and photos you see are the same ones on the restaurant's own counter — never inflated, never outdated.

  • Menu prices verified weekly with every partner restaurant
  • No pay-to-rank listings — search results are never for sale
  • A share of every subscription funds local-restaurant marketing grants
